Privacy

HoloTrack is a Pokémon card price tracker run by one person, Alex, in Zürich.

If you sign up for the e-mail: we keep your e-mail address, when you signed up and when you confirmed. It is used only to send the weekly HoloTrack e-mail. It is never sold or shared. Every e-mail has a one-click unsubscribe, and unsubscribing stops all e-mails.

Instagram and YouTube: HoloTrack's own accounts are connected only to publish HoloTrack's posts. No one else's data is read or stored.

This site sets no tracking cookies.

Questions or a request to delete your address: message @holotrack on Instagram.

Data, not financial advice.
